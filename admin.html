Example: AYET = 1 → 1 JPYC = 1 AYET.   MARU = 10 → 1 JPYC = 10 MARU.   MMK = 100 → 1 JPYC = 100 MMK.
Only the owner wallet can change rates; the contract rejects anyone else.
