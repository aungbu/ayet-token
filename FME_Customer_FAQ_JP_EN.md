## Transactions

### Q: Why did my transaction fail with "nonce too low"?
A: This usually means a transaction was sent with a nonce lower than the network expects —
often from sending multiple transactions quickly or a pending transaction. Check your
wallet's pending transactions, wait for them to confirm, and retry. If it persists, contact
FME Support with your transaction hash.

### Q: My transaction is stuck as pending. / トランザクションが保留中のままです。
A: [FME to complete: safe guidance — check the explorer with the tx hash, wait for network,
possibly resubmit with higher gas. Escalate if it doesn't resolve.]

### Q: Why did my transaction revert? / トランザクションが失敗したのはなぜですか？
A: A revert means the contract conditions were not met (e.g. insufficient balance, not
approved, or a contract rule blocked it). Verify the transaction hash and network on the
explorer. If it's an admin action (minting, pausing, upgrade), that requires FME Support.

## When to contact FME Support (escalation)
Contact FME Support for: lost keys or wallet compromise, minting/pausing/upgrade/ownership
requests, treasury or multisig actions, production/node issues, suspected fraud, or anything
unclear. TrueAI will help you prepare a support case with the safe details (project, network,
public wallet address, tx hash, time, error) — and will never ask for private keys, seed
phrases, or passwords.
